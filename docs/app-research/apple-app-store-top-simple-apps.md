# Apple App Store: top apps that are simple enough for FAS

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Scope:** Apple App Store (US), all non-game categories · **Audience:** FAS product and catalog planning

> Revised 2026-10-15: corrected notification/push capability (PWAs support Web Push incl. iOS 16.4+ home-screen apps).

## Summary

Most of the top of the App Store is out of FAS's reach. AI assistants, social networks, banks, streaming services and carrier or smart-home companion apps all depend on a network effect, a content library or native hardware. The categories just below that top are a different story. Utilities, Health & Fitness, Business, Finance, Food & Drink and the paid charts are full of single-purpose tools: document scanners, calorie and cycle logs, quote-of-the-day apps, day counters, 2FA authenticators, habit trackers, invoice makers, tuners, timers and calculators. Many have **hundreds of thousands to about 2 million ratings**, and many earn money through **weekly or annual subscriptions** for work a browser can do locally.

These are FAS's best targets. A solo developer can rebuild each one as an offline, client-side PWA in days. "Free, no tracking, no paywall" is a strong pitch against the subscription-heavy charts: scanner, cleaner and QR apps have a documented history of aggressive weekly subscriptions. Cycle tracking and journaling are privacy-sensitive by nature.

Reminders are not a blocker. Web Push works for web apps added to the Home Screen on iOS/iPadOS 16.4+, which is the normal install path for an FAS app, so "remind me at 8pm" apps (cycle, habit, fasting, to-do, subscription renewal) fit. The genuine platform-wide gaps are Home Screen widgets and alarm-clock behaviour (ringing through a locked phone or silent mode).

## Methodology and sources

- **Live charts (pulled 2026-10-15):** US iTunes RSS feeds `topfreeapplications`, `toppaidapplications` and `topgrossingapplications`, top 50 each, for these genres: Utilities (6002), Productivity (6007), Photo & Video (6008), Music (6011), Lifestyle (6012), Health & Fitness (6013), Finance (6015), Education (6017), Food & Drink (6023) and Business (6000). They were cross-checked against the apps.apple.com chart pages. Ranks below are written as, for example, "Grossing Utilities #1" or "Paid H&F #1", and they are a snapshot of that one day.
- **Ratings:** the iTunes Search API (`averageUserRating`, `userRatingCount`) for the US store, pulled the same day. **Rating count is used as the main proxy for lifetime demand.** Apple does not publish download counts. As a rough rule only, ratings are often around 1–3% of installs; that ratio is an industry heuristic and is not measured here.
- **Apple's 2025 year-end "most downloaded" lists (US)**, as reported by MobileSyrup, MacTech and GSMArena.
- **Sensor Tower** quarterly US/EU blog snapshots for 2025 (weekly figures). **Appfigures** 2025 insights. Press reports (TechCrunch, AppleInsider, Forbes via TechCrunch). Third-party estimators (ScreensDesign, AppGoblin), whose figures are rough and do not state a method.
- **Estimates are labelled as estimates.** Where no public revenue figure exists, a top-grossing or top-paid chart position is given as the demand signal instead of a number.
- **"Simple" filter:** a solid version can be built by one developer in days, mostly client-side, with no dependency on a content library, network effect, licensed media or native OS hook.

## Ranked archetypes

Abbreviations: Utilities, Productivity, H&F (Health & Fitness), Edu (Education), F&D (Food & Drink), Biz (Business). "Free #n", "Paid #n" and "Grossing #n" are positions in that category's US chart on 2026-10-15. "★4.84 / 1.94M" means a 4.84 average rating from 1.94 million ratings.

**Cross-cutting iOS-PWA caveats** apply to every row and are not repeated:

- **Push notifications (Web Push)** work for PWAs added to the Home Screen on iOS/iPadOS 16.4+ (the normal FAS install path), delivered even when the app is closed. **Badging** (`navigator.setAppBadge`) is available too. The platform already sends VAPID web push in production; `@freeappstore/sdk` has no documented `fas.push` helper yet, so an app uses the standard Web Push API with its own VAPID key, and a timed reminder needs something to send the push at the right time. **Recommendation:** a first-class `fas.push` / scheduled-send primitive (the platform already has `sendWebPush`) would make reminder apps trivial.
- A push notification is not an alarm: it cannot ring loudly through a locked phone or override silent mode.
- Home Screen widgets are unavailable.
- Storage can be evicted. iOS may clear data for a site that has not been opened for a while, so offer sync to per-user KV or a JSON export.

## Not buildable on FAS (and why)

| Archetype (example top apps) | Demand evidence | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| **Photo / storage cleaners** (Cleanup, Swipewipe, Cleaner Guru, AI Cleaner, CleanX) | Grossing Utilities #1, #4, #9, #11 and #32. Cleanup has 737k ratings. Swipewipe projected more than €10M/yr. | A PWA cannot enumerate or delete from the iOS Photos library and cannot see device storage. A file-picker "review selected photos" tool cannot delete anything, so it misses the point. |
| **VPN / proxy clients** (Shadowrocket, NordVPN, ExpressVPN, Proton, Stash, Loon, Quantumult X) | Shadowrocket is Paid Utilities #1 and #1 or #2 on Apple's 2025 paid iPhone list. NordVPN is Grossing Utilities #5, at about $1.7M/week US peak (Sensor Tower Q1 2025, estimate). | Needs the native NetworkExtension API and server infrastructure. |
| **Safari content blockers and extensions** (Wipr 2, Noir, Dark Reader, AdGuard Pro, UnTrap, Tampermonkey) | Paid Utilities #2, #4 and #19. Paid Productivity #17, #21 and #26. | Safari extensions must ship as native apps. |
| **Screen-time / app blockers** (Opal, BePresent, SocialLite) | Grossing Productivity #35 and #44. Opal ★4.72 / 90k. | Need the Screen Time / FamilyControls API. FAS can only offer a focus timer (#31). |
| **Alarm clocks** (Alarmy, Grossing Lifestyle #26, ★4.76 / 249k) | High. | A Web Push notification can arrive at a set time, but it is a notification, not an alarm: it can't ring loudly while the phone is locked, override silent mode, or guarantee an exact-second wake. Unreliable alarms are worse than none. |
| **Step counters / walking rewards / sleep trackers** (StepsApp, Stompers, WeWard, Scrambly, AutoSleep, HeartWatch, RISE, HealthFit) | Several in the H&F top 25. AutoSleep and HeartWatch are Paid H&F #2 and #3. | Need HealthKit, background motion or Apple Watch data. |
| **Universal TV remotes and screen mirroring** (TV Remote – Universal Control, Grossing Utilities #3, ★4.44 / 623k; many clones) | One clone estimated at about $2.3M (ScreensDesign, period not stated). | Need local-network discovery (SSDP/mDNS), raw sockets and AirPlay/DIAL. Safari cannot do this. |
| **Second phone numbers / call blocking / call recording** (Burner, Sideline, Robokiller, TapeACall, TrapCall, Truecaller) | Grossing Utilities #7, #8 and #16. | Need telephony carriers, CallKit or call-directory extensions, and paid number provisioning. |
| **Security suites / identity monitoring** (Norton 360, McAfee, Aura, LifeLock) | Grossing Utilities #6 and #15. Finance #9 and #12. | Need native device APIs and data-broker or credit-bureau feeds. Mostly a VPN bundle. |
| **Device / item trackers** (Find Air, Find Phone) | Grossing Utilities #20. | Bluetooth scanning in the background. Web Bluetooth is absent on iOS Safari. |
| **AI chat, AI photo and video generators, AI tutors** (ChatGPT, Gauth, Remini, Cal AI's scan model, PictureThis, Rock Identifier) | They dominate the free and grossing charts. | Value comes from server-side models. FAS has no server AI. A **BYO-key** wrapper is possible (as in #17, #22 and #30) but is not a mass-market substitute. |
| **Plant and rock identifiers** (PictureThis ★4.80 / 1.12M, Grossing Edu #3; PlantIn, Botan, Rock Identifier) | Very high. | Identification is a proprietary model plus a curated database. Only feasible as BYO-key LLM vision, at lower accuracy. |
| **Bank-linked budgeting and credit** (Rocket Money auto-cancel, Copilot, Monarch, Credit Karma, Dovly, Experian) | Grossing Finance #3, #5, #6 and #7. | Need Plaid-style aggregators, server compute and credit-bureau data. The manual version is in the main table as #19. |
| **Automatic mileage logging** (MileIQ, Grossing Finance #1; Everlance; Gridwise) | High. | Automatic trip detection needs background location. A manual trip log with OSRM distance is buildable, but that is not MileIQ's value. |
| **Fax / print / e-sign** (FAX from iPhone, FaxFree, Printer App, Docusign) | Several in the Grossing Business top 25. | Fax needs a telephony gateway, printing needs the native printer API, and legally binding e-signature needs an audit-trail service. |
| **Language-learning courses / book summaries / MasterClass** (Duolingo, Babbel, Pimsleur, Blinkist, Headway) | Grossing Edu #1, #6, #11, #20 and #31. | The value is the content library and curriculum. |
| **Dating, smart-home, carrier, bank and streaming apps** | They dominate the Lifestyle, Utilities and Finance free charts. | Network effects, hardware, or accounts held by the provider. |
| **Professional photo, video and music apps** (Procreate, LumaFusion, FL Studio, CapCut, Lightroom, Moment Pro Camera, NightCap) | Procreate is Apple's #1 paid iPad app of 2025. | Heavy native rendering and camera APIs (manual exposure, RAW, long exposure), with bundles far beyond 300 KB. |
| **Medical / clinical references** (Monash FODMAP, Pedi STAT, Epocrates-style) | Monash FODMAP is #4 on Apple's 2025 top paid iPhone list. | The value is proprietary, lab-tested or licensed clinical data. |
