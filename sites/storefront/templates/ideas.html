How to take an idea

  1. Pick an idea. Press Copy build prompt on a card. It copies a brief for your AI coding tool with the leaders to beat and the FreeAppStore features to use.
  2. Scaffold it. Run fas init <name>, or open VibeCode and paste the prompt.
  3. Publish it. Run fas check and then fas publish. Your app is listed here and the idea card links to it. See Get started.
