Web apps vs native apps

Why everyday apps work better on the web, and why that makes them free.

A native app is installed from the App Store or Google Play and built separately for each platform. A web app runs in the browser from a link. Modern web apps, often called progressive web apps (PWAs), can also be added to your home screen, open full screen, and work offline when they are built to.

Where native still wins

Some things are still easier natively: deep operating-system integration, some background tasks, and features a browser doesn't expose yet. Browser support also varies, especially on iOS. FreeAppStore lists exactly which browser capabilities apps can use, and every listing shows the permissions an app asks for.

Why it matters

For most everyday apps, such as timers, calculators, notes, trackers, learning tools, and creative tools, the web can do the job. Because web apps are so much cheaper to make, especially with AI, they don't need to fund themselves with ads or subscriptions. That is what FreeAppStore's mission is about: rebuilding those everyday apps for the web, free for everyone.
