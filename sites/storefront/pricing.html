External services

AI and other APIs accessed with your own key are billed by the provider under your account. VibeCode needs a usable AI key in your vault or a platform-configured supported provider connection. Free hosting does not include an unlimited AI allowance.

What FreeAppStore does not expose

There is no per-app server, direct SQL database, hosted file-upload API, custom-domain API, or arbitrary cron job API. Browser file pickers, on-device storage, and scheduled user push reminders are different features and are available where the browser and app support them. FreeAppStore apps take no payments.
