Delete your account

How to delete your FreeAppStore account, and what happens to your data.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This page covers the FreeAppStore account used by freeappstore.online, the apps published on it, and the FreeAppStore companion apps for Android and iPhone. The developer is FreeAppStore. One account covers all of them, so deleting it applies everywhere.

Delete it yourself

  1. Open freeappstore.online/app/settings?delete-account=1 in any browser. You do not need the companion app installed.
  2. Sign in with the same method you used before (GitHub, Google, Apple or email link).
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm twice.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. It also signs you out of every app and phone.

If you cannot sign in

Email support@freeappstore.online from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". If you signed in with GitHub, include your GitHub username. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before deleting it.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (login, name, avatar, email, date of birth if given).
  • Data apps saved for you: per-user storage, documents you own, roles, friendships, and app logs.
  • API keys in your key vault, push subscriptions and reminder schedules, votes and idea claims, and your VibeCode build sessions.
  • Photos you uploaded, removed by a cleanup job that runs every 15 minutes.
  • Companion apps: every connected phone, its per-app location permissions, and all location points stored on FreeAppStore, plus any connection in progress.
  • Desktop app and Contribute: every connected computer, pairing codes, and the records of what you contributed: your uploads, Good / Not good reports and notes, and attempt records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Apps you published and their public GitHub repositories stay online. Unpublish them first if you want them removed. Shared counters in apps are also kept, as they are not tied to you.
  • Backups: companion location data is never included in backups. The daily database backup does include other account records, such as your profile, per-user storage, documents, roles, friendships and logs, so copies can remain there after deletion. These backups do not yet have a fixed retention period.
  • Contribute audio that was accepted stays public in the app it was made for, without your name. If an app's creator blocked you from contributing, that block keeps your user id until the app is removed.
  • On your phone: the companion's 30-day sharing history stays on the phone and is never uploaded. Remove it with Disconnect this phone or Clear sharing history, or by uninstalling the app.
  • On your computer or phone: Contribute history, kept audio and the voice model stay on the device. Sign out or Disconnect removes the history and audio; Delete model removes the model.

Delete only companion data

To stop sharing without deleting your account, open the companion app and turn off an app, which deletes that app's location points on FreeAppStore, or choose Disconnect this phone, which deletes the phone's connection and all of its points. Location points are kept on FreeAppStore for 7 days at most in any case. See the privacy policy for details.
