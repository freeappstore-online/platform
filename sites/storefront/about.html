We're rebuilding everyday apps for the web

Free apps with nothing to download, no ads, no trials, and no subscriptions.

Why the web

Native apps are expensive to build, review, and ship to every app store, so they earn that money back from you. You download an app and it shows you ads. You pay $4.99 up front, or you get a 7-day trial and then a subscription.

The web is much cheaper to build on and run, and AI makes building cheaper still. One codebase works on phones, tablets, and computers, and a link is all it takes to share it. Because the apps cost so little to make, they can be free. That is cheaper for the people who use them and for the people who build them.

Who pays for it

The platform's running costs are covered by our sister store, ProAppStore, which earns its revenue from Pro apps listed on a subscription basis. Free apps and their users never pay. Read how FreeAppStore is free and how web apps compare with native apps.

What that means for you

Nothing to download

Open a link and the app works in your browser. You can add it to your home screen where your browser supports PWAs. Offline behavior depends on the app; sign-in, sync, and live data need a connection.

No ads, trials, or paywalls

FreeAppStore apps take no payments and don't show ads. Checkout SDKs and payment APIs are blocked when an app is checked and hosted. External APIs you choose to use with your own key can have provider charges; the app should explain those before you connect.

Open source

Every app's code is public on GitHub under the MIT license. Anyone can read it, report an issue, or improve it.

On your device or connected

Standalone apps can keep data on your device. Connected apps can use platform sign-in, cloud storage, public collections, and realtime rooms.

Clear permissions

App listings show the browser permissions an app requests. Read our privacy policy for account data, storage, analytics, and integrations.

Browse the catalog for timers, calculators, notes, learning tools, creative apps, and more. Each app runs on its own subdomain.

Cheaper for creators too

Publishing on FreeAppStore costs nothing. Creators get a public GitHub repository, an HTTPS subdomain, an automated deployment workflow, and a storefront listing. Publish through the fas CLI, or describe your app to VibeCode and let AI build it with your own AI provider connection. Provisioning is self-service; there is no application queue.

The free SDK includes sign-in, per-user key-value storage, public document collections, shared counters, realtime rooms, app roles, friendships, email, scheduled push reminders, API proxying, and an encrypted key vault. React apps use the SDK's Shell, hooks, and UI components. See capabilities and service limits.

FreeAppStore and the sister stores

FreeGameStore hosts browser games, and FreeWebStore hosts one-page sites. They have their own publishing tools and rules.

ProAppStore is a separate paid platform. Its plans and supported features are described on its own website. A FreeAppStore app does not automatically have a corresponding Pro version or a shared data migration.

Build or contribute

Have a question? See the FAQ. Get started, read the developer docs, or explore the platform source. Report app bugs and feature requests on the individual app's repository; SDK, CLI, hosting, and storefront issues belong on the platform repository.
