Frequently asked questions

What FreeAppStore is, how it stays free, and how to build for it.

General

What is FreeAppStore?

FreeAppStore is a store of free, open-source web apps for everyday tasks. Our mission is to rebuild everyday apps for the web, where they are much cheaper to build and run, so they can be free: nothing to download, no ads, no trials, no subscriptions.

Is it really free?

Yes. FreeAppStore apps take no payments and show no ads. There are no trials, premium tiers, or subscriptions. Publishing an app is free too.

How is FreeAppStore paid for?

Web apps cost far less to build and run than native apps. The platform's running costs are covered by its sister store, ProAppStore, which earns its revenue from Pro apps listed on a subscription basis. See How it's free.

Will an app start showing ads or ask me to pay later?

No. Apps can't take payments: the publishing check rejects payment and checkout SDKs and app hosting blocks the browser's Payment Request API. Ads are against platform policy.

Using apps

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open an app's link and it runs in your browser. Where your browser supports it, you can add the app to your home screen like a native app.

Do apps work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Apps are responsive and run in modern browsers on phones, tablets, and computers. Some browser features vary by device; see capabilities.

Do apps work offline?

It depends on the app. Many keep working offline once loaded. Sign-in, sync, and live data need a connection.

Do I need an account?

Not for most apps. Standalone apps keep data on your device. Apps with sync, sharing, or multiplayer can offer sign-in with GitHub, Google, Apple, or email.

What data do apps collect?

Every listing shows the browser permissions an app requests, and every app's source code is public. Apps can't bundle tracking SDKs directly. Read the privacy policy for accounts, storage, analytics, and integrations.

Can an app cost me anything?

Only if you choose to connect an outside service, such as an AI provider, with your own API key. That provider bills you directly, and the app should say so before you connect.

Building apps

How do I publish an app?

Install the CLI with npm i -g @freeappstore/cli, then run fas login, fas init, fas check, and fas publish. Or describe your app to VibeCode. See Get started.

What does it cost to publish?

Nothing. You get hosting, an HTTPS subdomain, a GitHub repository, automatic deployments, a storefront listing, and the SDK backend for free. Building with AI uses your own AI provider connection.

Can I build an app with AI?

Yes. Use VibeCode in the browser, or Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and other AI tools through the FreeAppStore MCP server. See Build with AI.

Can I charge for my app?

Not on FreeAppStore. Paid apps belong on ProAppStore.

Is there an approval queue?

No. Publishing is self-service. Apps must pass the compliance check (fas check) and be MIT licensed.
